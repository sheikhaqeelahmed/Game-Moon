Planet Scrappers

Nothing lasts. Everything has value.

Humanity mined planet Kessler-9 until its air plant failed, then left. The atmosphere leaked away, leaving a grey, cratered world covered in rusting factories, trucks, robots and starships, with the blue homeworld Halcyon hanging in the black sky. A salvage company called OmniSalvage Ltd. bought the whole planet for one credit, and you are its newest and cheapest employee. Take everything apart, sell it by the kilo, and build the biggest scrap operation in the sector.

Work order 01

The core loop

Every system serves one cycle. The prototype proves the first four steps feel good. Expand and Automate are designed now but built later.

01DismantleHit objects with your tool. Panels dent, bolts fly, the frame gives way.
02CollectScrap pops out and is pulled into your backpack by a short-range magnet.
03SellTip your backpack into the Recycling Depot hopper and watch credits count up.
04UpgradeBuy a stronger tool or a bigger backpack from Engineer Nova.
05 · laterExpandUnlock new zones with bigger, richer salvage.
06 · laterAutomateConveyors, crushers and robots do the work while you go bigger.
Work order 02

Art direction

Stylized, chunky and readable. Think toy-like industrial hardware that has been left in the rain for fifty years. Low-to-mid poly models with simple painted textures, so it runs on mid-range PCs and stays cheap to produce.

Chunky proportionsBig heads, big hands, big bolts. Tools are oversized so every hit reads from across the yard.
Rust tells the storyOrange-brown rust patches, faded hazard stripes and stencilled serial numbers on everything. Rust is a shared texture set, not painted per object.
Damage you can readEach object has 3 visible damage stages: dented, stripped, bare frame. You always know how close you are.
Colour means valueGrey iron, orange copper, green electronics, violet rare alloy. Players learn to spot loot by colour alone.
Grey world, rusty machinesBlack sky, pale grey cratered ground and a blue homeworld overhead. Against that, rusty orange machines and colour-coded scrap stand out at once. Working technology glows cyan.

Work order 03

The crew

The four characters, now based on your reference images. Each card says what we keep from the reference and what we change to match the brief. The three humans share one skeleton, so Mixamo animations, suits and tools work on all of them.

PLAYER

Rookie Scrapper

You · junior salvage operative

Curious, hard-working and a bit clumsy. Silent in dialogue; the personality shows through animation, like a tool bouncing off something too tough with a loud bonk.

  • Orange suit and round orange helmet with a fully black visor, so the player has no fixed face
  • Grey ear caps, black harness, belt and gloves, chest pockets, padded knees
  • Small oxygen backpack, heavy black boots
  • Mechanical salvage arm on the left, built from gears and pistons
Proposed gameplay hookTools clip onto the salvage arm. The rusty wrench is the first attachment; every upgrade from Nova replaces it with something bigger. The upgrade is visible on the player and in first-person view.
Change from the briefThe helmet is orange, not dark grey. We follow the reference.
COMPANION

SCRAP-BOT

Companion · tutorials and scanning

A floating helper that follows you, scans objects and runs the tutorial. Helpful, sarcastic and permanently unimpressed.

  • White head with a black screen face and cyan glowing eyes
  • Yellow ear discs, yellow chest armour, cyan core light
  • Tapered floating body, black ball joints, flipper arms
What we addThe eyes are drawn on the screen, so they change shape for each mood: eye-roll, squint, hearts at a big sale. The scanner beam shines from the chest core, and small claws fold out of the flippers when it grabs scrap.

Sample line: “Scanning… that truck is 80% rust, 20% regret, and some copper.”

NPC · UPGRADES

Engineer Nova

Upgrade Station · tools and machines

Smart, confident and quick to fix anything. Runs the Upgrade Station and later unlocks machines and automation.

  • Long red hair, determined expression
  • Armoured suit with grey shoulder plates, wrist guards and a utility belt
  • Heavy grey work gloves and boots
  • Backpack with a radio antenna
Proposed changeRecolour the suit to teal with orange armour plates, as in the brief, so Nova doesn't look like the player. Add a flip-down protective visor for workshop scenes.

Sample line: “Bring me copper and I'll build you a hammer that respects you.”

NPC · CONTRACTS

Captain Bolt

Salvage manager · missions

Big smile, bigger ambitions. Hands out missions and contracts. Looks friendly; the greed comes out in what he says.

  • Round, cheerful face and messy brown hair
  • Orange coverall with teal patch pockets and knee patch
  • Leather harness and tool belt hung with gadgets
  • One yellow robotic hand, one knitted fingerless glove, mismatched boots
Proposed changeAdd the radio headset and a gold OmniSalvage badge from the brief, plus yellow hazard shoulder pads so he reads as the boss. The round orange-and-yellow disc behind him becomes the company's sun logo on his office wall.

Sample line: “Every bolt you loosen goes toward my bonus. Our bonus. The company's bonus.”

Decision needed · colours Three of the four references wear orange. From a distance, players need to tell the player, Nova and Bolt apart. Recommendation: the Rookie keeps solid orange as the player colour, Nova moves to teal, and Bolt keeps orange with teal patches but gets yellow shoulder pads.
Licences · reference images These images guide the look. They are not game assets yet. They come from third-party sites (Tripo AI, IconScout, Fab and a research paper). We only put a model in the game if its licence allows commercial use; otherwise we model an original in Blender to match. Each one goes in the licence register either way.
Work order 04 · starter zone

Rust Valley

An old freight yard in a shallow crater. Grey regolith, crater rims and a perimeter fence close it in. Halcyon fills the sky and distant factory silhouettes make it feel larger than it is. The playable area is about 220 × 140 m: walking from base to the far side takes around 40 seconds.

Vehicle GraveyardWhere the first vehicle gets dismantled. Scooters with the wrench, Mule-7 trucks with the hammer.
Container YardContainers and crates give lots of iron. Vending bots hide electronics.

Work order 05 · roadmap

Beyond the valley

Four more zones, each built around a bigger tool and a new kind of salvage. Only Rust Valley is in the prototype; the others appear as distant skyline and locked gates.

Iron District

Unlock: Electric Dismantler (T3)Mood: grey smog, sodium lights

Abandoned factory blocks. Assembly lines, presses and robot arms that you strip room by room. Introduces indoor salvage and the first conveyor belts.

Spaceport Graveyard

Unlock: Plasma Cutter (T4)Mood: blue night, landing lights

Crashed freighters and broken landing pads. Huge hulls are cut apart section by section. Ship cores drop large amounts of electronics and rare alloy.

Deep Core

Unlock: Industrial Crusher (T5)Mood: dark tunnels, violet crystal glow

Underground mines and drilling rigs. Oxygen matters here. Rare alloy shows up in raw veins next to the old machinery.

The Mega-Factory

Unlock: Automated Salvage Robots (T6)Mood: furnace red, endless scale

One building the size of a city. The final contract: bring the whole thing down using everything you have built, including your own automated recycling lines.

Work order 09

Sound direction

Sound is half of what makes a hit feel good, so it gets priority in the prototype.

Layered hitsEach hit is a tool sound plus a material sound (thin sheet, thick plate, glass, circuitry), slightly varied in pitch so repeats don't get tiring.
Break momentA creak, a crunch, then a scatter of bolts. Scrap pickup is a rising "tink" that climbs in pitch as you collect a streak.
Cash registerSelling plays a mechanical counter that rolls up with the total. The single most satisfying sound in the game.
AmbienceNo air means no wind. You hear your own breathing, suit servos, radio hiss and clanks carried through the ground. Calm and lonely, never silent.
MusicRelaxed lo-fi industrial: soft beats, pads and tuned metal percussion.
SourcesRecorded or from Freesound (CC0 only, or CC-BY with credits), edited in Audacity. Every file logged with its licence.
